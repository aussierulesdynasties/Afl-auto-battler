Gate fixes (lead, 2026-11-20, "Send all four"):
1. Seats: painted grid gone. Stepped concrete treads (a row per 0.8 m), a 3D seat every 0.5 m (pan, lip, backrest, pedestal), an aisle every 12 m, teal-green as in aerial4. Pavilion banks are stepped rows too.
2. Textures: all 31 sit beside a crop (drone5, aerial4, grandstand photo, 2024 new-seats photo, 2023 Centre West site photo, and Google Earth 2026, labelled, where no photo shows the part). Colours set from the crops.
3. NE scoreboard: the tower rises from the centre of the round base about 2 m behind the board (GE l225, l270); blue front frame (GE l045), white back frame (GE l225). The SW scoreboard is added on the SW building: 13 m screen, dark side panel, maroon back posts, white top beam.
4. Bracing is modelled steel on the Northern and East-South backs (columns at each truss and mid-bay, girt, top beam, X brace every third bay). The crest is redrawn from the photo as a tall red rampant lion with black outline; the photo crest is about 6 x 14 px, so shape and colours are copied, not fine detail.

Left out as ads: the red panel beside the UTAS sign, club logos on the NE board back. Centre West rests on the satellite and grandstand photo only (current GE shows it demolished).

No open faults.
