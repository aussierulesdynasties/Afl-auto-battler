## 1. Pivot House, 71-77 Gheringhap St
- Read: https://www.pta.com.au/news/celebrating-craft-and-context-brick-inlay-brings-a-welcoming-presence-to-geelongs-pivot-house ; search snippets from pellicano.com.au/project/pivot-house-geelongvic
- Architect: Peddle Thorp. 17 storeys per PTA and Pellicano; OSM says 13 (conflict). Completed about 2023. Quest serviced apartments are on the lower and middle floors, build-to-rent flats above.
- Lower levels: "Rustic Red" brick. Street level is traditionally laid brick with a hit-and-miss (perforated) texture. The upper brick look is factory-laid brick-tile inlay.
- Upper levels: Hebel (AAC) panels. Finish colour UNCONFIRMED.
- Window layout, crown and balconies: UNCONFIRMED.

## 5. Old Geelong Post Office, 83 Ryrie St (corner of Gheringhap)
- Read: search snippets of VHD places/20473 and 4699 and Wikipedia Old_Geelong_Post_Office.
- Two storeys, rendered (stuccoed) brick, late Victorian Italianate (one source says Second Empire). Architect J.H. Marsden, built 1889-90.
- Ornate corner clock tower at Ryrie/Gheringhap, clock added 1910-11. Clock faces 2.5 m in diameter, hour hand 1 m, minute hand 1.25 m.
- Render colour and roof: UNCONFIRMED.

## 6. TAC HQ, 60 Brougham St
- Read: https://www.indesignlive.com/projects/tac-headquarters-by-mgs-architects ; snippets from hickory.com.au and mgsarchitects.com.au

- Precast concrete. The west facade has aluminium sun-shield panels in a mix of green, blue and red (artist Mathew Johnson). Ground-floor public arcade.

## 7. St Mary of the Angels Basilica, 150 Yarra St
- Read: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Mary_of_the_Angels_Basilica,_Geelong ; snippets from intown.com.au and historicalragbag.com. The VHD place page returned 403.
- Gothic Revival in bluestone. Length 61 m (Wikipedia) or 220 ft = 67 m (blog). Width 40 m overall (Wikipedia page); the search snippet said a nave width of 26 m. Transept to transept 126 ft = 38 m.
- Height: 64 m to the top. The bluestone spire itself is 46 m, the tallest bluestone spire in Australia. Phosphor-bronze cross, 2.4 m.
- History: nave 1872. From 1930 the transepts, crossing, apsidal chapels and the tower and spire (1931-37).
- Spire end and compass direction, spire shape (octagonal or broach), nave height, roof material and colour: UNCONFIRMED. A photo caption mentions a "south view", which is not proof of orientation.

## 9. Barwon Water HQ, 55-67 Ryrie St
- Read: https://architectureau.com/articles/barwon-water ; snippets from ghd.com and revitalisingcentralgeelong.
- 1977 building re-skinned by GHD Woodhead (2018 national commercial architecture award).
- Double-glazed curtain wall with a patterned metal sunscreen skin ("draped skin", water-drop motif, modelled on sun angles). Corten steel plaza wall and stair. Retained brick and bluestone buildings flank it. Rooftop garden and terrace.
