# Marvel Stadium exterior: parts and materials read off the 2019 loop (S/sv/loop/sheet0-6.jpg)
Local frame: s south+, t west+. North = s<-90, south = s>95, east = t<0, west = t>0.

## Bowl (all round, between towers)
- Ground: white round columns (~0.8 m dia) on a regular bay, gate numbers in red/white collar bands near the top.
- Ground storey: dark glazed shopfront/turnstile line set back behind columns; white-grey low-poly "web" panels (v20,v73).
- Level 2: curtain glass, dark-grey mullions, horizontal transoms, bays matching columns below.
- Two silver-grey horizontal cladding bands (aluminium composite, joints visible), one with a slim window strip.
- Top: teal-green steel brackets under the roof edge; white curved roof fascia; roof truss teal-green (lattice arch).
- MARVEL STADIUM sign boxes: red "MARVEL" box + white "STADIUM" on black, on the upper band (N, E, S-E faces).

## Stair/service towers (navy, ~8 around the bowl)
- Navy-blue painted cladding, panel joints in a grid, slightly darker navy plinth band ~2.5 m.
- Big mesh-glazed stair openings (dark steel grid over visible zig-zag stairs).
- Silver-grey vertical louvre screens on some faces (v25,v27,v70).
- Timber doors (vertical boards) flanking gates; white picket barrier at gates; purple "Medallion Club" canopy (v04,v15).
- Silver cylinders (2-3 tanks, ~3 m dia, ~8 m tall) at towers v09/v41/v45/v63/v65/v67.
- Mural on N tower (v16/v17: basketball/footy players); brick "New York facade" set piece with fire escape (v68/v69).

## North end
- Bowl as above; green ribbon sculptures (v18, v23).
- Ground: pale concrete paving with dark-grey drainage strips radiating; green painted paths (v22,v23).

## East side (Wurundjeri Way / car panos)
- Hoarding with murals along the road in 2019; City Edge 2024 (see S/dl25) is the "today" source here.

## South end (2019 = old Southside glazing; rebuilt as Stadium Square, use 2024 photos S/dlarch)
- Huge cantilevered silver box over white columns (v00-v02, v74-v75), LED screen (v75), glazed Southside wall.
