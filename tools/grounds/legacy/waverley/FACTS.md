# Waverley Park (VFL Park), 1990-1999 facts (sources read 2026-11-23)
- Legacy mode: 240 matches, 1990-1999 (21-27 a season).
- Field 200 x 160 m, "the biggest in the AFL" [W]; whether field or fence to fence is not said. [A] gives no size.
- K.G. Luke (members') stand: first level done for the 1970 opening; Harold Freedman mosaic mural 1986 [W].
  Public reserve stands round the rest of the ground, finished 1974 [W]. Seating only partly under cover [W];
  [A]: "Arena roof: No".
- Capacity 72,000 at the end [W, A]; record crowd 92,935 (1981) [W, A].
- Lights from May 1977 [W]; tower count and height not in [W] or [A] (data/grounds.json says 4, unsourced there).
- 1982: monochrome video scoreboard, the "VFL Park Superboard" [W]; [A] "a two-coloured video screen". Size not given.
- Sold to Mirvac Dec 2001; demolition began 11 Dec 2002 [W, A]. Kept: the members' stand (eight bays of the
  Sir Kenneth Luke Stand, top-level seating) and the oval, heritage listed 7 Sep 2000 (H1883) [W]. Hawthorn's base
  opened early/March 2006 on an oval "reduced from its original size" [A].
- Not published in what was read: goal-end orientation, stand, roof and tower heights, tiers of the public stands.
Sources: [W] en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waverley_Park; [A] austadiums.com/stadiums/waverley-park.
