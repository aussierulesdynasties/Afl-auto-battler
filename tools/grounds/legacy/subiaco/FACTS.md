# Subiaco Oval 2000-2017 facts (sources read 2026-11-23)
- Field 175 x 122 (Wikipedia, austadiums); fence to fence 191 x 132 (Wikipedia); goals east-west.
- West end: Three Tier Stand (Orr-Simons-Hill), 1969, tallest building. NW: Two Tier Stand 1980. Members wing two-tier 1981.
- Opposite members: Town & Country / ANZ / NAB stand 1995, two tiers + boxes. South side: big electronic scoreboard/video screen over southern stand (sold to Penrith 2018).
- East City End stands 1999-2000, two and three tier, precast, cantilevered roof hung from perforated columns, polychromatic brick shrouds.
- Subiaco club building NW corner 1971, two storeys near-flat roof.
- Light towers: 4 corners, 1997, 65 m, fan gantry four tiers (inHerit PDF 9e42f005, summary).
- Gates 1935 limestone/brick/terracotta Art Deco, cnr Roberts Rd & Haydn Bunton Dve (Gate 19, retained).
- Kitchener Park east: raised lawn, event car parking, goals on south side. Car parks west. Roberts Rd south, buildings to road reserve. Subiaco Rd north, rail line north.
