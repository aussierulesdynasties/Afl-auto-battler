# Accor Stadium (Stadium Australia), oval mode 2002-2015: measurements (not started beyond imagery)

Legacy mode plays 45 matches here, 2002-2015 (three or four a season) and one in 2022.

## Imagery and its limit

Google Earth web historical imagery (`tools/grounds/legacy/ge_history.js`, `LAT=-33.84737 LON=151.06335 R=950`):
30 dates from 3 Sep 2003 to 30 May 2016. Reference only, never committed.

The roof covers the seating bowl and overhangs the field: from straight above only the octagonal opening shows, so
the ends of the oval and most of the fence line are hidden under the roof in every frame. The turf edge cannot be
traced from these aerials the way the other grounds' are.

## Plan

- The stadium still stands, so the roof, stands and arches come from Google Earth's 3D mesh (status-bar readout, as
  at Ikon Park and Waverley).
- The fence: from oblique Google Earth 3D views solved on the painted field lines (`tools/grounds/ge/ge_solve.py`),
  or from period broadcast-angle photos. Whether today's lower seating (the mesh) matches the 2002-2015 oval mode is not yet checked.
