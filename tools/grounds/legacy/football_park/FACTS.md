# Football Park 2001-2013 facts (sources read 2026-11-23)
- Goals north-south. Inside the boundary 165 x 135 m; fence to fence 177 x 145 m; boundary to fence about 6 m; about 1.5 m fall from centre to fence (the "hump") [W].
- Members grandstand (west), original 1974; bench seats added in the bowl.
- Northern Grandstand opened 2001 (A$12.5 m, about 7,000 individual seats), extending the members' upper balcony level round toward the north end and the video screen [W, A].
- South end: Park Pavilion Suites ("Superboxes", 1997) on the southern concourse, from the outer tunnel/scoreboard round to the members [W, F].
- Outer: concourse roof 1982; aluminium benches on the lower deck 1982, plastic bucket seats from 2004 [W, F].
- Scoreboards: original scoreboard over the tunnel, south-east corner; video screen 1998 at the north (north-east per W); southern screen 2007 with a new scoreboard under it [W, F].
- Four light towers, 1983-84; height not published [W, F].
- Capacity 51,240 from 2004. Adelaide's training facility (2009, "the Shed") on the east side; Max Basheer Reserve east [C].
- Not published anywhere read: stand heights, tier counts, tower height. These are measured (shadows, photos).
