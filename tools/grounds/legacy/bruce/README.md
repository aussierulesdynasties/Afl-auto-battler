# Bruce Stadium (Canberra), sources

Legacy era: one AFL match, 27 May 1995 (Fitzroy v West Coast, `data/legacy/fixtures.csv`), on the oval. The oval was rebuilt as a rectangular
stadium in 1997-98, so nothing of it survives to measure today. No model exists yet.

## What imagery exists (checked 2026-11-26)

- Google Earth historical imagery here starts 22 Dec 2002, after the conversion. No use for the oval.
- ACT Government orthoimagery (data4.actmapi.act.gov.au, folder ACT_IMAGERY_MGA2020) starts with 2004_05.
- NSW Spatial Services historical imagery (HistoricalImagery19xx tile services) does not cover the ACT
  (404 at Bruce and at Queanbeyan for every year 1985-2001).
- The ACT's footprint index of historic aerial film
  (services1.arcgis.com/E5n4f1VY84i0xSjy/.../ACTGOV_Historic_Aerial_Photo_Footprints) lists frames over
  Bruce from **April 1995**, a month before the match (run 10, photos 210 and 211; 153 mm lens, 1,625 m above ground, about 1:10,600)
  and March 1997 (run 10 photo 136, run 11 photo 104), plus 1990, 1991 and 1993. No public scans of these
  frames were found; ACTmapi's own imagery page lists nothing between 1955 and 2004.

## What is needed

A scan of the April 1995 frame (or 1993/1997) from the ACT Government's survey and mapping team, or
dated photos of the 1995 oval. Without one of those, the oval's size and shape cannot be measured, and
data/grounds.json keeps the default size (no published AFL size was found).
