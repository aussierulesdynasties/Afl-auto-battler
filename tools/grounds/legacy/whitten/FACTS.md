# Whitten Oval (Western Oval), 1990-1997 facts (sources read 2026-11-23)
- Legacy mode: 84 matches, 1990-1997 (8-9 a season to 1993, 15-18 in 1994-96 with Fitzroy, 1 in 1997).
- Named Western Oval to 1995, Whitten Oval after [W]. Condemned before round 1, 1997; farewell match round 21,
  1997, Footscray v West Coast, 26,704 [W]. AFL-era capacity about 25,000 [A, W]; record 42,354 (1955) [W, A].
- "Particularly long and narrow with deep squarish pockets"; the wind strongest at the Geelong Road end [W].
- Today "160 x 130 m" [A], not dated; the field was later resurfaced, re-sized and regraded [W]. The 1990s size is
  not published in what was read.
- The EJ Whitten Stand was demolished from July 2022 and replaced by 2024 [W]. Lights only from the 2024 works [W, A].
- Not in what was read: the 1990s stands' names, sides, roofs, heights; scoreboard; goal-end compass direction.
Sources: [W] en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitten_Oval; [A] austadiums.com/stadiums/whitten-oval (and /redevelopment).
