# WACA Ground, 1990-2000 facts (sources read 2026-11-23)
- Legacy mode: 55 matches, 1990-2000. West Coast used it 1987-2000, Fremantle from 1995 [W].
- 1984-88: realigned and resurfaced [W]. Prindiville Stand, three tiers (named 1985); Lillee-Marsh Stand, two tiers
  (1988); Inverarity Stand (1970, the "Test Stand"); Farley Stand (1931) [W]. Sides and roofs not stated.
- Six light towers, 1986, $4.2 million; the floodlights "are 70 metres high" [W].
- 1954 scoreboard (still working) [W].
- Record AFL crowd 34,317, preliminary final 24 Sep 1994 [W].
- 2002: arena cut "by a total of 31 metres at the eastern and western boundaries", so football could no longer be
  played; Farley Stand and the old players' pavilion demolished, seats square of the wicket became grass hills [W].
- Not in what was read: field size, stand heights. austadiums.com refused the fetch.
