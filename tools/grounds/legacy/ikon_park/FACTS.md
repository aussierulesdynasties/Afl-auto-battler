# Ikon Park (Princes Park / Optus Oval), 1990-2005 facts (sources read 2026-11-23)
- Legacy mode: 242 matches, 1990-2005 (198 in the 1990s). Last AFL match Carlton v Melbourne, 21 May 2005 [W].
- Six grandstands, about 35,000 at its highest [A, W]. Named in the sources: Alderman Gardiner Stand (1909-13, iron,
  cast-iron columns), Robert Heatley Stand (opened 7 May 1932), Pratt Stand, Carlton Heroes Stand [W].
- Legends Stand, east end: begun 1995, opened 25 April 1997; with it the ground was roofed all the way round [W, A].
  So 1990-1996 had no roofed stand at the east end; that end's look before 1995 comes from photos only.
- Two western grandstands removed after 2005 for the training and administration building (2010) [W, A].
- Names: Princes Park, Optus Oval from late 1993 [A] / Nov 1993 [W body] (infobox says 1994).
- Not published in what was read: field size, stand heights, tiers, light towers, scoreboard. Measured instead.
