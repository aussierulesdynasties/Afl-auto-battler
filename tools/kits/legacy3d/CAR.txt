# Carlton's past home guernseys (1990-2025) for the 3D match player, one line per footyjumpers
# picture (tools/kits/kits.txt), each copied from match and club-shop photos (URLs above each line;
# footyjumpers pages are www.footyjumpers.com/carltonhome.htm and www.footyjumpers.com/images/<name>.gif).
# Format and ops: tools/kits/kits_today.py. Sponsors, maker and AFL logos left off; the woven CFC
# monogram is drawn (tools/kits/marks/car_*.png, white = the mark).
#
# Colours, sampled from the photos (clubs.csv's #002B5C renders royal blue; every photo is near-black navy):
#   navy #211D30   1995 Grand Final replica (thecarltonshop), chest median
#   navy #262C40   2010 round 5 match photo (Judd), the best-lit match photo; used 1998-2020, which
#                  have no product shot (the other match photos are under-lit, #0E0E13 to #040507)
#   navy #1E2329   2024 Puma replica, body median;   navy #292D38   2025 Puma replica, body median
#   #0E0E14        the shadowed inside of a V-neck (2001 and 2018 match photos)
#   #0C141A        the darker navy V insert under the 2009-13 crew collar (2010 round 5, Judd)
#   #9FA1AD        the 2001-06 monogram, silver (median of its pixels in the 2003 round 2 photo)
#   #E6E6EA        the 2014-19 monogram's bevel: the photo's bevel/face ratio (#CCCAD2 / #E2E0E5) on white
#   #C6CCD3        the 2024 replica's white collar lining; #9BA2B2 the 2025 replica's lining (in shade)
# Numbers white, no outline (every back photo).
#
# Positions: y 0 the top of the shoulder by the neck, 1 the hem, read off fraction grids on the photos.
# In match photos the hem is hidden, so the top of the shorts is read as 0.86 (the 3D calibration);
# product shots give the true hem. Monogram height and place from the photos; its width keeps the traced
# mask's own width-to-height (each mask is traced from the real monogram; on the 1990 and 2024 replicas
# the monogram measures 0.98 and 0.93 wide per unit high, as their masks do). The 3D body shows a unit of
# cell x 1.07 times narrower than a unit of cell y (measured on the captures), so the drawn width is
# mask ratio x height x 1.07. V-necks: the opening is drawn in the shadowed
# inside navy, as FIT.txt does; crew necks are left to the collar binding.
#
# Masks: car_1990_cfc (1995 GF replica, 4x, thresholded), car_1998_cfc (Carlton-1998.gif, 8x, the
# navy detail lines kept), car_2007_cfc (Carlton-2007.gif, 8x), car_2014_cfc + car_2014_cfc_bevel (face
# and face-plus-bevel, Carlton-2018.gif, 8x; the 2016 photos show this ladder monogram with its bevel,
# not the footyjumpers 2014 drawing's older one), car_2020_cfc (2024 Puma replica, 3x).
