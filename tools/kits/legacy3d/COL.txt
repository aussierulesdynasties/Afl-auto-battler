# Collingwood's past home guernseys (1990-2025) for the 3D match player, one line per footyjumpers
# picture (tools/kits/kits.txt), each copied from photos of the real guernsey (sources above each line).
# Format and ops: tools/kits/kits_today.py.
# Colours, sampled from the photos: black #111111, white #F2F2F2 (1998 framed B #141317 / W #CED2CA,
# 1998 mannequin B #070707 / W #DFDFDF, 1990 wool replica B #0B090A / W #D7D3C8, Sharkey 1990 B #1B1C16 /
# W #ECEAE8; the 2026 line's #141414 black). Shorts black at home in every photo (white shorts are the
# clash pair).
# Positions as kits_today.txt: y 0 the top of the shoulder by the neck, 1 the hem (armpit about 0.34,
# shorts hide below about 0.86); x 0..1 seam to seam as you look at that side. Read off flat framed
# guernseys where one exists, otherwise off match photos (y calibrated on the shorts line = 0.86).
# Where a photo and the footyjumpers drawing disagree, the photo wins; 2009, 2011 and 2013 have no
# usable photo and follow the drawing only.
# Two layouts: 1990-2000 is a WHITE guernsey with three black stripes (black centre, white sides);
# 2001 on is a BLACK guernsey with three white stripes (white centre, black sides). refs.md's "9 bands,
# black at both sides" for 1990-2000 was the black armhole bindings: every zoomed photo (Sharkey,
# McCartney, the 1990 replica, the 1998 framed and mannequin items) shows white side panels.
# Sponsors, maker and league logos left off. Club emblems not drawn: every one is a small crest
# (0.14 of the width at most, 2017's 125-year crest the largest), as the 2026 line leaves its crest off.
