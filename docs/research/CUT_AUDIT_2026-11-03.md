# Cut audit, 2026-11-03 (plan step 2b)

Every screen and every control the player can touch, marked against the director's order:
**1 story generator (the coach's career) > 2 deep sim > 3 approachable with agency.**
Nothing here is cut; the director decides. Survey of origin/main at 7680b65a.

Status (2026-11-03, docs/roadmap/32-director-decisions-2026-11-03.md): candidates 1-5 shipped, 9 and 11 approved and
cut; the rest are undecided. The tables below are the survey as taken.

Verdicts:
- **DECISION**: a real choice that changes outcomes or the story (sim code read to confirm).
- **CLICK**: navigation, confirmation or viewing; no choice.
- **NOISE**: a choice whose effect is absent, invisible or too small to matter.

Frequency: **W** every week (~23 rounds + finals), **S** once a season, **C** once a career, **R** rare/optional.
