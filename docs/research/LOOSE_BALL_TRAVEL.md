# Where a loose ball goes: measured from AFL broadcasts

_2026-11-17. Source for `MatchSim.LOOSE_TRAVEL`. Director: "the visual sim needs to marry up to
the match sim"; "the match sim needs to produce a visual sim which resembles real football"._

Each row is one real loose ball, measured frame by frame from a full-match broadcast:
where the ball came loose, and where it was next gathered (or stopped). The engine
picks one row of the right kind at random each time a ball comes loose, and the replay
rolls the ball to that spot. There is no formula: the rows are used as filmed.

- **Up**: metres toward the goal the side that lost the ball was attacking (minus = back toward its own goal).
- **Across**: metres across the ground, left minus, as that side attacks. The engine also uses each
  row's mirror image (the ground has no left or right).
- **Seconds**: from the ball coming loose to the gather.
- **Tackle spill** = the ball knocked out of a tackled player's hands (the footage's "spill").
- Distances are sized from ground markings (centre square 50 m, centre circle 10 m, goal square 9 m,
  posts 6.4 m) where in shot, otherwise from player height (about 1.88 m). Most rows are low confidence
  (tight, panning shots): the size of each is right to within a metre or two, not centimetres.

6 more loose balls were filmed but could not be sized (only a tight or ground-level shot): COSK23-03, COSK23-04, COSK23-07, SKCO24-13, SKCO24-14, SKCO24-21. They are left out.
