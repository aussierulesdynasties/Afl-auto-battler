# Era dramas and year events, 1990-2026

`dramas.csv`: 86 events. Each row has the season it fires (`season`), the years it spans, the clubs, what happened, the real penalty, its kind, whether it is about personal conduct, a game hook, and a source. Text marked UNCERTAIN is unverified, and so are sources that disagree with each other. Figures come from web sources (Wikipedia, afl.com.au, club sites, AAP, SEN), not primary AFL records.

How the rulings apply:
- Every controversy (cap breaches, drugs, tanking, betting, conduct) sits behind the new-game switch "Controversies: on or off". Penalties land as they did in history.
- `personal_conduct = yes` (14 rows) means name the club only. The summaries already do this. Sources may still name the people involved.
- Fitzroy follows the director's merger rule (see its game_hook).
- Every hook changes only club decisions, the list, money, morale, the board or the press. Renames, jumpers and ground looks are news stories only.

## Years with no event, and why

- **1994**: nothing season-specific was found or verified. Fremantle's licence (awarded around then) fires as its 1995 entry instead.
- **1998**: the only candidate is the AFLPA reaching 100% membership, which is not an event a coach acts on.
- **2004**: no verified off-field drama. A 2004 pre-season sexual assault allegation was left out on purpose (see gaps).
- **2006**: the Seven/Ten rights deal for 2007-11 was not verified. Hawthorn's Tasmania deal already fires in 2001.

## Gaps and cautions

- **Left out on purpose (sensitive):** St Kilda's 2010 leaked photos (they involved a minor), the 2004 St Kilda sexual assault allegations, the 2015 death of an Adelaide coach, and individual mental-health stories such as the Bulldogs' 2025 split with a former No. 1 pick. The 2026 Sydney hotel row is included but flagged: the police case is open and it involves an alleged sexual assault, so the director should rule on it.
- **Unverified:** the mid-1990s players' strike threat (one source, no year); when Essendon's 1990s cap penalty was announced; how many Fitzroy players the Lions took; the years of the Kangaroos' Sydney games; the round of Carlton's 2015 sacking; the year St Kilda returned to Moorabbin; North's first Hobart season.
- **Penalty figures sources disagree on:** Carlton's fine ($930k or $987.5k) and whether it lost 2004 picks; the Adelaide CEO's suspension (6 or 12 months); the exact draft picks Essendon lost in 2013-14.
- **Not researched yet:** expansion concessions for each new club (Fremantle, Port, Gold Coast, GWS), the 2006 and later TV rights deals, other ground renames (Football Park, Kardinia Park, Carrara and Subiaco naming rights), the 2023 umpire-dissent crackdown, Geelong's 2026 concussion row and the Kolodjashnij inquiry (both named at the 2026 finals launch, no details found), AFLW-specific dramas, and smaller cap fines from the late 1990s and 2000s (Collingwood, Hawthorn, Richmond, the Bulldogs, Geelong, North Melbourne, Brisbane, St Kilda).
- **Footscray's 1989 Fightback** falls just before 1990, but it could seed a 1990 start ("the club that nearly merged last year").
