# Brainstorm: the economy and budget side (2026-11-13)

Director, 2026-11-13: "research and brainstorming session me for how to overhaul the economy/budget side of the game".

This brief is research and an interview. Nothing here is decided until his answers below say so.

## 1. What the game has today

Two kinds of money, both football money. There is no club bank account, revenue, profit or debt.

### Where it is thin or invisible to play

1. **The department budget is one screen, once a year, and then gone.** You set three dials in the off-season; nothing during the season reminds you or asks again. The effects are real but small (10-15%) and diffuse.
2. **It breaks parity.** Rivals cannot invest (rule 8.C.23: "Any mechanic that improves the human club needs an equivalent AI route"; director 2026-11-09: every club gets every buff). This is a defect to fix in any direction below, not a question.
3. **The cap never binds.** Payroll sits at 91-93% of the cap (Director Bible open question 9.18, "make a good list cost more?", unanswered). A cap that never pinches is not a decision.
4. **Money has no story.** No club is rich or poor, no club is in trouble, the board never talks money, a premiership changes nothing on the books. The bible's first pillar (story, legacy, dynasty) has no money thread.
5. **Staff cost nothing.** Hiring the best assistant is free, so there is no "can we afford him" moment. The coach-market revamp (separate TODO row, being added elsewhere) will touch this; this brief only notes the link.

### Rulings that bound this work

- **Club profit and loss were ruled out of scope on 2026-10-05**: "Match payments, ASAs and club profit/loss are out of scope" (Director Bible 9.9) and "No club profit or loss expansion" (rule 7.97). Directions C and D below would lift that ruling; only he can.
- No microtransactions; money is a fictional football resource (rules 1.11, 7.79).
- Every club gets every buff; options equal, each with a real plus and minus (2026-11-09).
- No hidden AI resource advantage; rivals never act on what they could not know (4.63, 8.C.23).
- Numbers at the decision gate: upside, downside, size and odds, never which is better (2026-10-18).
- No contract-admin busywork; no six routine negotiations every off-season (7.97). No arthritis from clicking (bible principle 5).
- Money UI is "Meters and deal cards": the cap as a tappable bar, each contract or trade a two-sided card (2026-11-12, row 169).
- Legacy money in "Dollars of the time" (2026-11-12, row 142).
- The director found AFCM "overly complex and inscrutable, undermining trust in its numbers" (genre research, 2026-10-05).

## 2. Real AFL club money

Sources are linked inline. Figures marked "approx." come from press summaries, not club reports.

- **Revenue.**
  - The AFL paid clubs $420.8m in 2024: an equal base to every club, plus top-ups for clubs in need ([AFL 2024 result](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1266774/2024-afl-financial-result)).
  - Big club against small club: Collingwood earned $85m in 2024 and $96m in 2025, with 112,491 members ([Collingwood 2025](https://www.collingwoodfc.com.au/news/1935079/collingwood-announces-2025-financial-result)). North Melbourne earned about $53m (approx.).
  - The gap shows in sponsorship and crowds ([Footy Industry](https://footyindustry.com/afl/afl-and-club-finance/2018-club-finances)): in 2017, Collingwood and Sydney each took over $18m in sponsorship and Gold Coast under $6m. In 2018, West Coast took $34m from membership and gate, GWS about $4m.
  - Gaming machines (pokies): Carlton made $20.5m from hospitality and about 300 machines in 2024 ([Ausleisure](https://ausleisure.com.au/news/gaming-machine-revenues-continue-to-underpin-finances-at-afls-carlton-fc)). Melbourne lost about 10% of its revenue when it left pokies in 2018.
- **Salary cap (Total Player Payments).**
  - The current agreement runs 2023-27: the cap is $15.02m in 2023 and rises to $18.44m in 2027 ([Wikipedia, citing the agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_salary_cap)).
  - Minimum spend (the floor): 90% from 1987, 92.5% from 2001, **95% from 2013**.
  - Front-loading is allowed (St Kilda front-loaded Max King's extension). 58 players earned $1m or more in 2025 ([AFL](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1463887/the-money-men-2025-total-player-payments-revealed-58-players-paid-1-million)).
  - Clubs reportedly carry room between years, spending 95% one year and 105% the next (unverified).
  - Breaches: Carlton 2002 ($987,500 fine, lost picks, barred from the 2003 pre-season draft). Adelaide 2012 (the Tippett deal: $300k fine, first two draft rounds forfeited).
- **Football-department soft cap.**
  - It began in 2015 with a luxury tax on overspend ([The Roar](https://www.theroar.com.au/2014/06/04/afl-opts-soft-cap-footy-departments/amp/)): 37.5% for a first offence and 75% for a repeat, the tax capped at $1m and $2m.
  - COVID cut it from about $9.7m to $6.2m.
  - 2024: $7.275m. Up to $100k of unused room carries over, and 20% of the senior coach's pay sits outside the cap.
  - 2025: **$7.675m, with a 90% minimum spend** ([AFL](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1222813/2025-27-football-department-soft-cap)). Then +$750k for 2026 and +$350k for 2027 ([AFL](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1341365/coaches-coffers-boosted-after-months-of-soft-cap-angst)).
  - It covers coaches and football staff, not players. Rich clubs pay the tax; poor clubs struggle to reach the cap.
- **Going broke and being propped up.**
  - Fitzroy 1996: about $1.75m owed, administration, then an AFL-backed merger with Brisbane ([Lions](https://www.lions.com.au/news/14635/merge-day-20-years-on)).
  - Melbourne's "Debt Demolition" fundraiser cleared about $5m of debt in 2008.
  - The 2012-16 Club Future Fund paid out $144m to clubs in need.
  - Help now comes as draft picks and rookie spots: North 2023 ([AFL](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1041604/afl-commission-signs-off-on-north-melbourne-assistance-package)) and West Coast 2025. The AFL is building a formula for future packages ([AFL](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1513274)).
- **Facilities.** Mostly government money: stage two of the Western Bulldogs' Whitten Oval cost about $77m, over $46m of it from Victoria ([Premier of Victoria](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/completion-whitten-oval-win-melbournes-west)).

## 3. How other management games handle money

- **Football Manager.**
  - A slider trades the transfer budget against the wage budget. Staying in budget lifts board confidence ([FM24](https://www.footballmanager.com/features/smarter-transfers-squad-building-and-finance)).
  - Overspend lets the board sell your players. Financial Fair Play penalties and administration also exist.
  - Criticised: board cuts feel arbitrary, and a club is "near impossible" to bankrupt ([SI forum](https://community.sports-interactive.com/forums/topic/567336-suggestion-financial-implications-in-football-manager)).
- **Out of the Park Baseball.**
  - The owner sets the budget according to their mood. Revenue comes from gate, media and merchandise.
  - Criticised: setting ticket prices becomes fiddly number-tuning ([OOTP forum](https://forums.ootpdevelopments.com/showthread.php?p=3584655)).
- **Motorsport Manager.**
  - Sponsors are a risk trade: cash now, or a small fee plus big bonuses for set finishing places.
  - Praised: missing a target becomes a story moment, because next year's car depends on the money ([Autosport review](https://www.autosport.com/gaming/feature/7139/motorsport-manager-full-game-review)).
  - Facilities cost money to build and to run.
- **Basketball GM.**
  - It dropped spending ranked against other clubs ("like bidding in an auction on eBay") for a visible 1-100 level per line with diminishing returns ([finances revamp](https://zengm.com/blog/2023/06/finances-revamp/)).
  - The follow-up found coaching and health spending too strong ([bug](https://zengm.com/blog/2023/08/finances-revamp-bug/)).
- **Common thread.** Money is fun when it forces a few clear, painful choices with visible results: pay the star or the depth, take the risky sponsor. It is a chore when it means tuning numbers every week, or when the rules are hidden. That matches the bible: few gates, stated odds, no arthritis from clicking.

## 4. Candidate directions (as put to him)

| Direction | Adds | Costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Fix and show what exists: rivals allocate their budget too, money gets meters (row 169) | Parity (fixes a defect), readability | No new decisions |
| B. A cap that pinches, with real tools | Agency: who to pay, who to let go; a dynasty must be paid for | Balance runs, contract AI work, more to read per deal |
| C. A real football-department soft cap with luxury tax, staff wages paid from it | Real footy; "can we afford him"; rich and poor clubs | ClubBudget rework; rivals must spend by the same rule |
| D. Club wealth that moves with success, plus AFL help for clubs in need | Story and legacy: your dynasty builds a bigger club | A wealth number to model and balance; a brake so rich clubs can't run away |
| E. Full club finances (bank balance, debt, going broke) | The biggest story | CEO work; AFCM-style complexity; lifts the 10-05 ruling |

He chose B, C and D, kept the 2026-10-05 ruling (no bank, no profit or loss) and rejected E. A is a defect fix and goes ahead under any direction.

## 5. Interview (director, 2026-11-13)

Round 1
- Scope: **"Football money only"** (the cap, contracts and the football department; no club bank; the 2026-10-05 ruling stands).
- Club wealth: **"Real differences"** (same rules for all, different wealth, as in real footy).
- Salary cap: **"Pinch plus real tools"**.
- Where the coach meets money: **"Off-season screen, Pitch to the board, Press and board talk"** (not money cards mid-season).

Round 2
- Department money: **"Real soft cap + tax"** (one soft cap for all; each club's wealth decides how far it can spend; spending over the cap pays the luxury tax).
- Staff wages: **"Yes, from dept money"**.
- Cap tools: **"Front/back-loading, 95% spend floor, Carry-over room, Breach risk"**.
- Pitch to the board: **"Both"** (a refused pitch costs board confidence; a granted one raises the season's goal).

Round 3
- Wealth over a career: **"Moves with success"** (grows with finals and flags, shrinks slowly in lean years).
- Propping up clubs: **"Yes, picks and money"** (a club in need gets extra department money and priority picks by a stated rule, rivals and you alike).

### Applied from existing rulings, not asked

- Every club, rivals included, allocates its department money, pitches to its board, pays its staff and gets AFL help under the same rules (2026-11-09; rule 8.C.23).
- Every money decision states its upside, downside, size and odds, in dollars of the time, and never says which is better (2026-10-18; 2026-11-12).
- In Legacy, era rules follow history: no soft cap or tax before 2015, and the floor at its level for that year (2026-11-11 "real penalties land"; Legacy era rules L4). Real salary-cap rorts stay under the Controversies toggle.
- Money shows as meters and two-sided deal cards (row 169).
